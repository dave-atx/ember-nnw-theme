Ember

A warm, editorial theme for NetNewsWire that reads like a magazine, with big headlines and drop caps in Apple’s own fonts.

The details

The screenshots below are the same article in Ember (and a podcast episode), shown in whichever appearance (light or dark) your system is using.

  • Apple system fonts. SF Pro and New York, so there’s nothing to download and text stays sharp at any size.
  • Images at their own size. Wide photos span the column; small ones never get upscaled.
  • Real-world feeds. Ghost cards, WordPress galleries, MacStories app icons, etc lay out the way the publisher meant them to.
  • Tuned per platform. Follows Dynamic Type on iPhone and iPad (with a larger default on iPad). The Mac gets its own type scale.

On every screen

The same design everywhere NetNewsWire runs.

Install

  1. One click: on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad, tap Install in NetNewsWire above. NetNewsWire will open and offer to add Ember.
  2. Or install manually: download the latest release. On macOS, unzip and double-click Ember.nnwtheme. On iOS/iPadOS, open the .zip and share it to NetNewsWire.
  3. Select it: in NetNewsWire settings, choose Ember as your article theme.
